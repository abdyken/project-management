## Source and interpretation boundaries

- Paid UNT thresholds apply to local school graduates on paid contracts only; historical state-grant winning scores are not eligibility guarantees.
- The Kazakh Language and Literature subject combination (misgrouped on the EN page), per-subject minima, shortened college pathways, additional exams, and campaign applicability still need office confirmation. No scope is declared fully covered by the seed.
- General master CT minima are conditional on scientific/profile track and do not apply to MBA/EMBA without a programme-specific source. The 5.5 versus 6.0 IELTS conflict is not converted into a rule.
- Master credit counts are read from the programme-specific amended fee order where explicitly printed (including 60, 90 and 130 ECTS). A universal 120 ECTS assumption is not used.
- Bachelor MDE is sourced from Appendix 1, page 2. It is not applied to master/PhD programmes without their own evidence. The total degree estimate needs the actual professional/MDE split.
- Seed does not resolve duplicate code 7M01501 or silently add the omitted Mathematics (Science). Existing catalogue/FAQ review questions remain open.
- Evidence and quotes for each field and rule are stored in `backend/app/data/guidance.json`; staff changes persist in the database and are never overwritten by startup seeding.
